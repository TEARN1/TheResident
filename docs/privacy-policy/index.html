Privacy Policy

How Resident Crew handles your data

Effective 28 August 2026 · Applies to the Resident Crew web app and its Android package

🏘️ Resident Crew — neighborhood app

Resident Crew is a neighborhood-watch and community app: residents share alerts, chat, coordinate on a shared map, and connect with neighbors. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it.

01Information we collect

CategoryWhat it includesWhy we collect it
Account info Email address, display name, avatar To create and secure your account, and identify you to neighbors
Location Device GPS coordinates or manually entered suburb, resolved to an approximate area via OpenStreetMap To show relevant local alerts and place you on the community map
Community content Posts, messages, gossip-feed items, notices, and trust-circle connections To power the core features you post and send
Safety reports Incident details you submit, and automated anomaly-scan metadata To flag and route safety-relevant activity to the right people
Subscription & billing Plan tier and payment status (handled by our payment processor — we do not store card numbers) To manage paid features
Device & usage Notification tokens, sign-in timestamps, basic diagnostic logs To deliver notifications and keep the service running and secure

02How we use it

  • Operate core features: the community map, alerts, messaging, trust circles, and organization broadcasts.
  • Send notifications you've opted into — safety alerts, replies, and community updates.
  • Detect abuse and security issues, including automated scans for suspicious activity.
  • Maintain and improve reliability, and diagnose bugs.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data to serve third-party advertising.

03Who we share it with

Content you post to a community, group, or public feed is visible to other members of that community by design. Beyond that, we share data only with:

  • Supabase — our backend and database provider, which stores account, location, and content data on our behalf.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — used to turn GPS coordinates into a readable suburb or city name. Coordinates are sent for this lookup only when you use the live-location feature.
  • Payment processors — for subscription billing, if you upgrade to a paid plan.
  • Law enforcement or authorities — only where required by law, or to protect the safety of residents.

04Location data, specifically

Live location is opt-in. It's requested only when you tap a location feature (like posting your position on the map), and your browser or device will always show its own native permission prompt first. You can decline and enter your suburb manually instead. You can revoke location permission at any time in your browser or device settings.

05Data retention

We keep account and community data for as long as your account is active. Safety and security logs are retained separately for a limited period to support abuse investigation. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain records by law or to resolve an open safety report.

06Your choices

  • Access & correction — update your profile, name, and avatar directly in the app.
  • Deletion — request account deletion from within the app's settings, or by contacting us below.
  • Notifications — manage or disable push notifications in your device settings.
  • Location — revoke location access at any time; the app falls back to manual suburb entry.

07Children's privacy

Resident Crew is intended for adult residents and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

08Security

We use industry-standard safeguards — encrypted connections, access controls, and database-level hardening — to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
